Hello, World

I'm Angel Siji Das

First-Year Animation Student  ·  2D Specialist  ·  Storyteller

Animation found me before I found it. Growing up, I was always the kid who watched cartoons frame by frame, pausing and rewinding to study how a character's hand curved, how their eyes telegraphed emotion a split second before the dialogue arrived. That obsession became a calling.

Now, in my first year of formal animation training, I'm turning that passion into craft. I specialise in 2D animation — the raw, beautiful discipline of bringing a character to life one drawing at a time. Every project I take on is guided by a simple belief: if an audience feels something, the animation has done its job.

Beyond traditional character animation, I work in Adobe After Effects to create polished motion graphics and product advertisements, and I love the pre-production side — sketching storyboards that lay the visual foundation for a story before a single frame is animated.

I'm based in India and available for freelance projects globally. Whether you need a short animated loop, a full lip-sync scene, a storyboard package, or a motion-graphics ad, I'd love to bring your vision to life.
