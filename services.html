Services

2D Character Animation

Frame-by-frame 2D animation is where my heart lives. I create expressive, fluid character animation using traditional principles — anticipation, follow-through, squash and stretch — applied digitally in Adobe Animate or Toon Boom Harmony.

Whether it's a short animated loop for your social media, a full animated sequence, or a character walk cycle, every frame is hand-crafted with intention.

Lip Sync Animation

Breathing life into dialogue through precise phoneme-matched lip sync. I study the audio waveform carefully, mapping vowels and consonants to mouth shapes that feel authentic rather than mechanical.

Combined with subtle facial acting — raised eyebrows, blinks, jaw tension — the result is a performance that your audience will believe.

Phoneme Mapping Facial Performance Dialogue Scenes

Storyboarding

A great story is planned before it's animated. I create clear, communicative storyboards that pre-visualise your narrative — establishing camera angles, character staging, shot transitions and emotional beats.

My boards include timing annotations and director's notes to give your production team everything they need to move forward confidently.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do you work with international clients? +

Absolutely. I work with clients worldwide. Communication is via email, WhatsApp or video call, and payments are accepted via international transfer or UPI.

What's your typical turnaround time? +

Turnaround depends on the scope. A short animation loop takes 3–5 days. A full storyboard package can take 5–10 days. We'll agree a clear timeline before work begins.

How many revision rounds are included? +

Standard projects include two rounds of revisions. Additional rounds can be arranged at an agreed rate.

What file formats do you deliver? +

Animation is delivered as MP4 (H.264), ProRes, or GIF depending on your needs. Source files (AE project, Animate file) can be provided on request. Storyboards are delivered as PDF and PNG.

Do you take on student or non-profit projects? +

Yes — I believe in supporting creative communities. Reach out and we'll find something that works for both of us.
